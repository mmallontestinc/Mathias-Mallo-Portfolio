# About Me

## My Approach

Operations first. Security-aware. Automation when useful. Human accountability always.

I work best where technical support, compliance, platform thinking, and practical engineering overlap. My focus is on solving root causes rather than only treating symptoms: if a workflow is repeated, risky, hard to audit, or difficult to explain, it is a candidate for better documentation, automation, or tooling.

## Professional Context

My path connects Uruguay, Spain, IT operations, technical support, compliance operations, and platform building. That background shaped the way I work: close to real users and real operational pressure, but with enough engineering depth to build internal tools when manual process becomes a bottleneck.

The projects in this portfolio come from real operational and compliance needs:

- Evidence management for audit and certification work
- Multi-system reconstruction of support and release history
- Secure access and documentation workflows
- Linux compatibility validation for specialized hardware
- SBOM and vulnerability visibility for internal software review
- AI-assisted engineering workflows with human validation and governance

## Working Principles

- Start with the operational problem before choosing a tool.
- Document the workflow so another person can understand and repeat it.
- Treat security, privacy, and redaction as design requirements.
- Use automation to reduce manual risk, not to remove accountability.
- Use AI as an accelerator for exploration, drafting, validation planning, and troubleshooting.
- Keep final ownership with the human operator, engineer, or reviewer.

## What This Portfolio Shows

This is not a collection of tutorial projects. It is a public-safe record of internal systems, validation frameworks, and compliance-supporting workflows that solved real business problems while respecting confidentiality.
