**Format:** Role-alignment case study

## Role Fit Summary

My recent work maps strongly to remote-first IT support, secure access workflows, documentation, compliance awareness, and practical ownership. Much of the work involved turning messy operational needs into documented, repeatable systems.

## Positioning

The strongest fit is for IT operations roles that value technical depth, compliance awareness, documentation discipline, and the ability to build tools when repeated manual work becomes a risk.
